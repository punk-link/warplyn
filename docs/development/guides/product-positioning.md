# Product positioning

Use this guide when writing product descriptions for Warplyn's landing page, README, repository description, and app About screen.

## Agreed wording

| Role | Wording |
| --- | --- |
| Motto | Your writing. Your voice. Your agent. |
| Product category | AI writing workspace |
| Assistant name inside the product | Editorial Assistant |

Use this product description with the motto:

> Warplyn is an AI writing workspace where an Editorial Assistant helps you develop and revise your Articles. You decide what changes.

Add open-source, local-first, and desktop details where relevant, as in the [README](../../../README.md). Keep the category and Assistant name consistent across product introductions. Use concrete instructions in task-specific UI and help text; repeat the motto only where it serves as product positioning.

## Author benefit and expectations

The description tells Authors what the product helps them do and who controls the result. Preserving their voice, reviewing proposed changes, and keeping recoverable history remain central to the product promise. Keep setup language approachable and descriptions of general workflows provider-neutral.

The motto expresses the relationship with the Assistant. The category describes the workspace Authors use. Retain "Editorial Assistant" for the named in-product Assistant.

Reserve "AI writing agent" as the main product category until the Assistant reliably carries a writing goal through several steps. Evaluate that wording against demonstrated behavior: working with Article context, carrying out the required steps, and returning proposed changes for Author review. Author approval remains part of the agent workflow; the term does not require unrestricted autonomy.

"AI writing workspace" makes a more limited promise about independent execution. Its tradeoff is that surrounding copy must explain the Assistant's value. For Author-facing introductions, explain the writing work directly rather than relying on familiarity with coding agents.

## Applying the wording

Use the agreed positioning when updating the landing page, README introduction, repository description, and app About screen. This guide records the wording decision; it does not establish that every surface has been updated. Keep existing capability and privacy claims accurate when adapting the description.
