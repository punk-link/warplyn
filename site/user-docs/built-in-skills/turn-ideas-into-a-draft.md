# Turn your ideas into a draft

Use **Turn ideas into a draft** when you have source material but need a coherent first pass. It develops supplied ideas into a straightforward narrative while preserving their meaning and avoiding unsupported claims.

Open **Quick actions**, choose **Turn ideas into a draft**, explain the direction you want, then select **Send editorial request**. With a selected passage, the request uses that passage and material you explicitly include in your message. Without a selection, it can use the current *Article* together with your direction.

::: tip Example
Select the passage where Ana opens her notebook and ask, "Help me draft one possible next paragraph using the details already in this passage. Keep the page blank and do not explain why the river seems to recognize her."
:::

The Assistant prepares a *Proposal* for review. Check its structure, claims, and wording before accepting any changes. It does not save or change the *Article* by itself. For a rewrite of existing prose, see [Improve flow and clarity](improve-flow-and-clarity.md).
