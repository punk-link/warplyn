# AI provider costs

AI assistance is optional. Warplyn does not bill you for AI requests. Your connected provider may charge your account when Warplyn sends a request.

## What affects the cost

Each provider sets its own rates and billing rules. Charges can depend on the provider, model, amount of *Article* text and instructions sent, and the length of the response. A subscription to a provider's chat service may not cover API use. Check the provider's current API pricing and billing terms before connecting a key.

Warplyn sends the selected text when you make a request with a selection. Without a selection, the request may include the whole *Article* and relevant context. Choosing a smaller scope or a model with rates that fit your needs can reduce costs, depending on the provider's pricing rules.

Routine *Article* work, such as smoothing a passage or making a focused revision, usually uses a modest amount of text and does not require an expensive model. Try a lower-cost model first. Use a more expensive model when a task's results show that it needs one.

## Check your usage

Use your provider's account to review API usage, charges, and any available alerts or spending limits. Warplyn does not show the provider's bill or enforce a spending limit. Contact the provider about its billing, refunds, or account charges.

In **Settings** → **AI assistant**, you can choose a **Default model**, an **App model**, and models for specific tasks. The model used for a request affects the provider's charges. See [AI providers and models](providers.md) for connection and model setup.

You can write and manage *Articles* without connecting an AI provider. Editing, saving *Revisions*, and using local backups do not send AI requests.
