COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers,
before you begin.

Will free features ever become paid?

No. Features offered for free will stay free. Warplyn will always remain open source under the MIT License. Optional extras may be paid.

Will Warplyn charge me for AI?

Warplyn does not bill you for AI usage. Your connected provider may charge for requests. See the supported providers. You can also write without an AI connection.

Does Warplyn upload or sync my Articles?

No sync service is provided. Articles and Revision history are stored locally. When you request AI help, only the relevant text and context are sent to your chosen provider. Read the privacy overview for more detail.

Can I use it for commercial writing?

The MIT License allows commercial use. Your writing remains yours.

Where can I get help?

Read the documentation or open an issue on GitHub. The community can help with setup and report problems.
