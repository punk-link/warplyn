LOCAL-FIRST

Your writing stays
on your computer.

Articles, drafts, and Revision history live in Warplyn’s local SQLite database. Warplyn doesn’t run a sync service or upload your writing.

When you ask the Editorial Assistant for help, Warplyn sends the relevant text and context to the AI provider you chose. The provider’s data policies apply.

YOUR TOOLS, YOUR CHOICE

Bring the provider
that works for you.

Connect a supported AI provider with your API key. Choose a model that fits your work and budget; available operations depend on its capabilities.

  • OpenAI
  • Anthropic
  • Google Gemini API
  • xAI Grok
  • DeepSeek
  • OpenCode Zen

OpenCode Zen also lets you use models from additional providers in its catalog. Available operations depend on the chosen model. AI features need an internet connection. Your provider may charge separately for its service.
